Pronouns

Clear rules, examples, Persian explanations, and practice.

1. What is a pronoun?

A pronoun replaces a noun so we do not repeat the same noun.

ضمیر کلمه‌ای است که به جای اسم می‌آید تا از تکرار اسم جلوگیری کنیم.

Ali is an engineer. He works in an office.

2. Subject pronouns

PronounPersianExample
IمنI am ready.
youتو / شماYou are kind.
heاو (مذکر)He is my brother.
sheاو (مونث)She is a teacher.
itآن / این (برای اشیا و حیوانات)It is new.
weماWe study English.
theyآنهاThey live here.

3. Object pronouns

me, you, him, her, it, us, them are used after verbs or prepositions.

She called me. / I helped him. / They invited us.

4. Possessive adjectives

SubjectPossessiveExample
ImyMy phone is new.
youyourYour book is here.
hehisHis car is black.
sheherHer name is Sara.
weourOur class is small.
theytheirTheir house is big.

Important: “its” shows possession; “it's” means “it is”.
