Verb To Be

Clear rules, examples, Persian explanations, and practice.

1. What is “to be”?

The verb to be is one of the most important English verbs. In the present, it becomes am, is, are.

فعل to be در زمان حال به سه شکل am، is و are استفاده می‌شود.

SubjectTo beExample
IamI am an engineer.
YouareYou are ready.
He/She/ItisShe is happy.
We/TheyareThey are students.

2. Negative

I am not tired. / He isn't busy. / We aren't late.

3. Questions

Are you ready? — Yes, I am.
Is she a teacher? — No, she isn't.

4. Practice

1. They ___ from Iran.
2. I ___ not tired.
3. ___ he your brother?
