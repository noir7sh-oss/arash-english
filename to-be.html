BEGINNER GRAMMAR

Verb To Be

The verb "to be" is one of the most important verbs in English.

What is "to be"?

We use the verb "to be" to talk about people, things, feelings, jobs and locations.

Am, Is and Are

Subject Verb Example
I am I am a student.
You are You are happy.
He is He is a teacher.
She is She is a doctor.
It is It is cold.
We are We are friends.
They are They are students.

Examples

✅ I am Arash.

✅ She is a teacher.

✅ They are students.

Negative Sentences

Add not to make a negative sentence.

I am not tired.

He is not a doctor.

They are not at home.
