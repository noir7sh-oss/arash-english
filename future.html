Future: Will & Going To

Clear rules, examples, Persian explanations, and practice.

1. Will

Use will + base verb for predictions, promises, offers, and decisions made at the moment of speaking.

I think it will rain. / I'll help you. / Don't worry, I will call you.

will + فعل ساده برای پیش‌بینی، قول، پیشنهاد و تصمیمی که همان لحظه گرفته می‌شود کاربرد دارد.

2. Be going to

Use am/is/are going to + base verb for plans and intentions, especially when there is evidence for a prediction.

I'm going to study tonight. (plan)
Look at those clouds! It's going to rain. (evidence)

3. Present Continuous for arrangements

For a fixed personal arrangement, English often uses the Present Continuous.

I'm meeting my friend at 7 tomorrow.

4. Negative and questions

I won't forget. / She isn't going to travel.
Will you come? / Are they going to study?

5. Quick comparison
