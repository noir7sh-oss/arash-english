English Vocabulary

Learn useful English words for everyday life.

Hello

Used when meeting someone.

Hello!

Friend

A person you know and like.

She is my friend.

Happy

Feeling good or pleased.

I am happy.

Learn

To gain knowledge or a skill.

I learn English.

Speak

To talk to someone.

I speak English.

Teacher

A person who teaches.

He is a teacher.
