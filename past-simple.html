A1–A2

Past Simple

Clear rules, examples, Persian explanations, and practice.

1. Use

Use the Past Simple for completed actions at a finished time in the past.

I visited Tehran last year. / She called me yesterday.

گذشته ساده برای کارهایی استفاده می‌شود که در گذشته انجام شده و تمام شده‌اند.

2. Regular verbs

Usually add -ed: work → worked, play → played, visit → visited.

Spelling examples: study → studied; stop → stopped; live → lived.

4. Negative

Use didn't + base verb.

I didn't go. ✓
I didn't went. ✗

5. Questions

Did you watch the movie? — Yes, I did.
Did she call? — No, she didn't.

6. Past of To Be

was = I/he/she/it; were = you/we/they.

I was tired. / They were happy. / Were you busy?

7. Practice

1. We ___ to the cinema last night.
2. He ___ his homework yesterday.
3. ___ you see Sara yesterday?
4. They ___ at home last night.
