A1–A2

Articles: A, An, The

Clear rules, examples, Persian explanations, and practice.

1. A / An

Use a before a consonant sound and an before a vowel sound.

از a قبل از صدای همخوان و از an قبل از صدای واکه استفاده می‌کنیم.

a book • a university • an apple • an engineer • an hour

Choose by sound, not only by spelling: a university starts with a /y/ sound, while an hour starts with a vowel sound.

2. The

Use the when the listener knows which person or thing we mean, or when there is only one in the context.

I bought a book. The book is interesting.
The sun is hot.

وقتی منظور ما برای گوینده و شنونده مشخص است یا چیزی یکتا است، از the استفاده می‌کنیم.

3. No article

We often use no article with plural or uncountable nouns when speaking generally.

Books are useful. / Water is important.

4. Common patterns

UseExample
one non-specific singular thingI need a pen.
specific thingWhere is the pen I gave you?
general pluralDogs are friendly.
general uncountableMilk is healthy.

5. Practice

2. I saw ___ dog. ___ dog was very small.
3. ___ English is spoken in many countries.
