A1–A2

Present Simple

Clear rules, examples, Persian explanations, and practice.

1. When do we use the Present Simple?

  • habits and routines: I work every day.
  • facts: Water boils at 100°C.
  • regular situations: She lives in Baku.
  • timetables: The train leaves at 8.

حال ساده برای عادت‌ها، کارهای تکراری، حقایق و برنامه‌های زمانی استفاده می‌شود.

2. Positive sentences

SubjectVerbExample
I/You/We/TheyworkThey work here.
He/She/ItworksShe works here.

He/She/It: usually add -s: work → works, play → plays. Verbs ending in consonant + y: study → studies. Some verbs use -es: watch → watches, go → goes.

3. Negative

Use do not (don't) with I/you/we/they and does not (doesn't) with he/she/it. After does, use the base verb.

I don't work on Friday. / He doesn't work on Friday.

4. Questions

Do you speak English? — Yes, I do.
Does she speak English? — No, she doesn't.

5. Frequency adverbs

always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never

I usually wake up at 7. / He is always late.

6. Practice

1. My brother ___ to work by bus every day.
2. She ___ coffee in the evening.
3. ___ your parents live here?
4. Which is correct?
